Achromia

AfflictionDate added: September 30, 2026

An affliction that, unlike a disease or illness, is rooted within the flow of Arcana, specifically attributed to the Oblivion sign. It presents itself either as a curse on those with dual or trinity Oblivion signs, or as an effect afflicted by the same group of people to others in one way or another.

While harmless if exposure is little (e.g. single Oblivion signs), continuous or extreme exposure is bound to cause irreversible damage:

  • Sensory deprivation
  • False memories
  • Loss of memories
  • Pigmentation bleaching or discoloring
  • Eventual total loss of one’s mind and cognitive capabilities
