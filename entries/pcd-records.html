Potens Caelestis Doomsday Records

Cosmology Date added: September 14, 2026

Description

Also known as Omens of the Celestial End-time or the Doomsday Records, this is a collection of theories and hypotheses derived from cosmological studies by followers of Conundrum and Liminality. The records depict various scenarios for the end of the Sublime Bubble, some more appraised than others.
