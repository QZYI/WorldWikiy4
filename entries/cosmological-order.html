Cosmological Order

Cosmology Date added: September 14, 2026

Onthontos, the Prime Cause

Before (non)existence, a single creator stood in a space with no definition, all alone with their thoughts. They eventually grew restless with themselves and their all-round will. And so they created “The Nothing” and placed the “Something” on top.

They then layered miracle upon miracle—concepts that shaped the cosmos. First came Quiddity and Lucidity through reification, giving the creation essence and making it perceivable. They filled everything with matter and constructed laws and principles to keep it predictable. Finally, they gave their creation life with the final miracle: Time, letting the world adjust itself before their gaze.

But no matter how much creation bloomed, they grew more restless. Along with the creation of time and space, they divided their consciousness across a multitude of divine expressions—Benevolence, Malevolence, Intellect, and Creation. These expressions shaped the cosmos for the Star Years to come, eventually clashing and dividing into individual deities: the Prime Causes. An insignificant part drifted away to create humankind.

Prime Causes

Also known as Prime Idols, the Prime Causes are unknowable beings formed from the dispersed avatar of Onthontos. Each holds unbound power. While each is said to have its own will, they never act directly, instead extending power to Archidols. Because of their nature, almost nothing is known about them; they are said to reside beyond the cosmological firmament.

Archidols

From arch- and idol, Archidols are considered the power ceiling of the cosmos within the firmament. Their power results from a liaison with their Prime Cause, yet remains as unimaginable as theirs. There is no definitive account of how Archidols are born. It is said that individuals whose inscapes’ Ethos spirals to such extreme degrees that it overpowers mortality may establish a bond with a Prime Cause.
