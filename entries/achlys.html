Epitome

Achlys is a Philanthropist Drifter possessing a trinity Oblivion bond and the only Etheric sign in her Seal. She originates from the isolated world of Naima, departing from it during the Starscourge Plague period to wander the cosmos for answers regarding the blight. Early on in her journey she would be struck with Achromia, but would nonetheless carry on with her journey. Soon after she would meet the afterimage, Daggon, through her chairvoyant background. This would eventually lead her to discovering The Birthstone Finality, which would fuse with her blade to become the sword Death—ultimately used to slay the archidol Exitium, Blight of Virulency, back at Naima and consequently end the Starscourge Plague.

Backstory

Prior to the events of the Starscourge Plague and its arrival to Naima, Achlys used to be a Funeral Mourner and a Chairvoyant. When the Dakk Dynasty invaded her remote hometown, Dharia, she volunteered to partake in the war as a frontier officer. Later becoming a sailor, she would drive out the bodies of the deceased to drop them in the Styxian River as a gesture of good faith.
