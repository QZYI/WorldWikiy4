Arcana Seals and Signs

CosmologyDate added: September 30, 2026

Signs

Arcana Signs are used to represent the underlying composition difference in Arcana. They act as a symbol for what they enact.

Seals

Seals are a bond of Arcana Signs cultivated by living and inanimate things. They are comprised of 3–5 signs.

Dual Signs

When 2 of the same sign are present in a seal, that means the thing bearing the seal is extremely potent and capable in utilizing that specific Arcana, much more so than seals with singular signs of that type.

Trinity Signs

The absolute ceiling to how many of one sign can be present in one seal is 3. While extremely rare, and especially volatile to cultivate, it is considered the closest point something can get to the power of an Archidol, excluding bearers of an Etheric sign (i.e. Achlys).

Arcana Signs List

Sinew, Insight, Oblivion, Epoch, Intellect, Etheric, Deceit, Bloodbloom, Tenacity
