Blighted Sinners

GroupDate added: September 30, 2026

Also known as Obsessors/Bishops of Virulency. They are Seal-Bearers that voluntarily submitted to Exitium’s Starscourge Sickness, fusing with it at a level that goes beyond a normal Blighted Soul. They forfeit their own will and self to serve Exitium’s goals so they can be fully assimilated and immortalized within them. Though in present day not many of them remain, with the second most powerful—Sunbleed—being slain by the Philanthropy Clause.

Blighted Sinners mentioned/present in the narrative

Sunbleed (Deceased), Cellentin, Rinmourd
