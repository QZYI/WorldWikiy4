Otherworld Courier Proxy

OrganizationDate added: September 30, 2026

Description

An affiliate vessel with the Stellar Commission Committee (SCC) and an adjunct to Scholars of Philanthropy. They aim to support the chase of a restored cosmos the Philanthropists have, while building themselves a name through SCC.

Crew

Considerably eccentric as some workers at CSS noted: the crew consists of people coming from varying backgrounds, but united by similar interests and goodwill.

The vessel is led by Huan with engineer, chef and janitor, analyst, and moral support—Indigo, Yanfei, Polias and Autumn respectively. Autumn is the crew’s moral support.
