Autumn

CharacterDate added: September 30, 2026
A child eons in preservation of a cradle drifting calmly in the expanse of space. Now on-board on a trade vessel with life beginning anew.

Epitome

Autumn is the protagonist. Her real name is unknown. She debuts in the Otherworld Courier Proxy’s vessel after its crew found her in suspended animation inside a sustain-box. She originates from the historical world of Cayahuanca, which was ravaged and annihilated by Apollyon some time in Star Year 7920. Autumn remains the sole survivor from that world 9000+ Star Years later. Her origin was uncovered by the crew later down the line after she exhibited severe symptoms of Achromia, the effect that any living being gets when they gaze at Apollyon.

Autumn remains mute throughout the entire narrative, having not spoken any word.

Backstory

Very little is known about her past. She claims to have been an Archidol, a “Universe Deus Ex Machina,” and an assistant baker to her mother in her journal that was present in the sustain-box. Though only the last is actually factual.
