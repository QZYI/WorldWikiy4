Starscourge Plague

Major Event Date added: Star Year 7,910

The Starscourge Plague was a two-parter major event that had reshaped the cosmos drastically. First initiated by Exitium during Star Year 7,910 due as a result of the Archidol's sudden madness.

Surfeit First

During the early stages of the plague, Exitium and their following, The Blighted Sinners, invaded and infiltrated countless worlds. Spreading many diseases all across. But most notably, the Starscourge Sickness.

At the late stages of the period, somewhere around Star Year 7,989. The Starscourge Sickness had completely wiped out 10,000+ worlds, infected 3.7 Quadrillion beings, converting them into Blighted Souls. And had rendered more 30% of habitable systems desolate through miasma. Around Star Year 8,004 the Starscourge Sickness and the Blighted Souls would begin to die down as Exitium's divinity fractures by their madness. A part of it splitting apart into a new archidol, Pothos. And the remainder of them would go inactive for a long time.

Surfeit Second

During Star Year 16,335, Exitium would unprecedentedly awaken and launch a second invasion throughout worlds and habitats in the cosmos. This time the severity would be much worse. Leaving only about 10% of worlds standing.

The madness would eventually be stopped for good when Exitium personally appeared in the world of Naima, ending it, but also being slain by Achlys with the sword Death.
