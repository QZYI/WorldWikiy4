Archidols

CosmologyDate added: September 30, 2026

Esoteric beings that exist as flowing Arcana throughout the Outermost Ley Line Web. They manifest in countless universes for various reasons but ultimately the same goal—to stay afloat through interaction with various universes, Idolionic Events. While those interactions may be mutually beneficial between a universe and an Archidol, others, such as Exitium’s Starscourge Plague and Allfinis’s Tide of Extermination, are entirely meant to extinguish entire universes. If an Archidol stays inactive for a prolonged amount of time, they are likely to be assimilated by other ones.
