Inscapes

Cosmology Date added: January 3, 2025

Inscapes are the culmination of one's qualities, cosmological attributes and personal philosophy. They are used to describe where and how a thing stands in the cosmos metaphysically.

They are divided into 2 parts—
Ethos (Character) and Quanta (Expression Potency).
The Ethos represents the unique composition of an individual, that includes their worldview and reason.

Quanta dictates the potency an individual can exert from bearing power of a divine expression. The nature often reflects the Ethos of the individual. Every Quanta Concentration has a hard restriction to how potent it can run, but most never reach that ceiling without intense external factors.
But for those who do hit it, it's possible for them to eventually break it or push it. Though it's unreasonably difficult to do so.
