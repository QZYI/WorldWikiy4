The Philanthropy Clause

OrganizationDate added: September 30, 2026

Also known as the Scholars of Philanthropy. A universally respected group of noble figures that wishes to restore the state of the cosmos before the events of The Starscourge Plague through aiding various worlds and people in need while hunting down the remainder of Exitium’s following.

In recent times they have gained a boost in reputation for slaying the infamous Blighted Sinner Sunbleed.

Affiliated figures

Achlys, Huan
