The Starscourge Plague was a two-parter major event that had reshaped the cosmos drastically. First initiated by Exitium during Star Year 7,910 due as a result of the Archidol's sudden madness. 

Surfeit First 

During the early stages of the plague, Exitium and their following, The Blighted Sinners, invaded and infiltrated countless worlds. Spreading many diseases all across. But most notably, the Starscourge Sickness. 

At the late stages of the period, somewhere around Star Year 7,989\. The Starscourge Sickness had wiped out 10,000+ worlds, infected 12.2 Trillion beings, converting them into Blighted Souls. And had rendered more than 30% of habitable systems desolate through miasma. Around Star Year 8,004 the Starscourge Sickness and the Blighted Souls would begin to die down as Exitium's being fractured by their madness. Going inactive for a long time. 

Surfeit Second 

During Star Year 16,335, Exitium would unprecedentedly awaken and launch a second invasion throughout worlds and habitats in the cosmos. This time the severity would be much worse. Leaving only about 10% of worlds standing. 

The madness would eventually be stopped for good when Exitium personally appeared in the world of Naima, where they were slain by Achlys with the sword Death.
