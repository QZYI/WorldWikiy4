# Apollyon

**[Sign Seal]** Sinew-Sinew-Oblivion-Oblivion-Oblivion 
**[Affiliation]** Not disclosed

*"THEY will scream and weep in HIS presence as HE alone descends in full light. HIS cosmic radiance shall be tampered into a furious cocktail and poured full strength into THEIR hollow bodies to shatter THEIR mind and will with a single gaze."*

— Excerpt from Doomsday Records attributed to Apollyon.

### Epitome
Apollyon is an uncategorized figure that roams the cosmos in intent to ultimately slay the archidols. He believes that they are pure evil and their influence is too far spread. He wants to do so by tearing apart the firmament that separates the cosmos from the Outermost Ley Line Web itself.
