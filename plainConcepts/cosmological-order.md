# Potens Caelestis Doomsdays Records

**Onthontos The Prime Cause**
Before (non)existence, a single creator stood in a space with no definition. All alone with their thoughts. They eventually grew restless with themselves and their all-round will.And so they created “The Nothing” and placed the “Something” on top. Then kept layering one after the other miracles (concepts) to shape the cosmos— Firstly putting Quiddity and Lucidity through reification to give essence to their creation and make them perceivable. Then they filled everything matter and constructed laws and principles to keep everything predictable.Finally they gave their creation life with the final miracle— Time, letting the world adjust itself before their gaze.But no matter how much creation bloomed, they grew even more restless. So along with the creation of time and space, they divided their consciousness across a multitude of divine expressions— Benevolence, Malevolence, Intellect, and Creation. Letting them shape the cosmos for the Star Years to come. Those Divine Expressions would eventually come to clash with one another, dividing into individual deities, Prime Causes. And an insignificant part would drift away to create the humankind.

**Prime Cause (Ruler of Divine Expression)**
Also known as Prime Idols. Unknowable beings formed from the dispersed avatar of Onthontos. Each holding unbound power. While each of them is said to have their own will, they never act directly. Instead, extending their power to Archidols. Due to their nature, almost nothing is known about them. They are said to reside beyond the cosmological firmament.

**Archidols**
From Arch- and idol. They are considered the power ceiling of the cosmos within the firmament. While their power is a result of a liaise between them and their Prime Cause, it is still as unimaginable as theirs. There is no definitive fact about how Archidols are born. Considerably it’s said that individuals whose inscapes’ Ethos spirals into such extreme degrees that it overpowers their mortality.
